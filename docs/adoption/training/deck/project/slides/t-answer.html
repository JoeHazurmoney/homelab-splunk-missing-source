One cause, many rows

Is homepc down?

Unknown. Its data passes through Cribl, and Cribl is not reporting. The host may be fine.

Fix first?

Cribl. Check its container or service, as the Action says. The rows behind it clear when it is back.

Tell whom?

The Cribl or platform owner. One ticket for the whole outage, not one per host.

Rule of thumb: when many rows share one cause, fix that cause first.

This was staged for training by stopping the Cribl container. Cribl’s own row went DOWN about 5 to 6 minutes after the stop; the host rows behind it first show their own cause, then switch to Cribl DOWN once Cribl itself is DOWN.
