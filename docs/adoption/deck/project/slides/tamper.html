TAMPER DETECTION

A stopped agent is a finding, unless something explains it

Explained

Planned Ansible work, or a host shutdown or reboot. No alert.

Not explained

One tamper alert, once the agent is back and nothing explains it.

Also watched

Log clears, auditd and rule changes, service installs, Cribl config edits.

Stopping the agent is the quickest way to blind a SIEM. The app pairs every stop with what could explain it: a maintenance marker posted by the automation before the change, or a host shutdown. The two amber rows are real stops made by hand in the lab, so they alerted once. When a tamper sign comes within 15 minutes of data going silent, the cause becomes “Possible deliberate blinding”.
