Two searches run every minute, so a status shows up to about 2 min after its limit is crossed. Limits come from the profile, a learned value or an override.

Measured in the lab’s fault-injection run: a stopped agent read DOWN in 4 min 4 s to 4 min 28 s; Cribl DOWN in 5 min 6 s; a 120-second item read MISSING in 5 min 4 s. An agent stop is held until the agent comes back, so a planned restart or reboot can explain it first. That is why its alert comes later.
