FEWER FALSE ALARMS

Each item has its own limit

Cadence profiles

Heartbeat, periodic, continuous, sporadic or info: not one threshold for all.

Learned limits

From 14 days of real gaps, after at least 3 days of history.

Holds, not pages

After a Splunk restart or a pause, statuses wait 5 min. No false alert.

A busy log and a nightly script cannot share one threshold. Each item has a profile, and for continuous and sporadic items the app learns the limit: 1.5 times the 90th percentile of its daily longest gaps, plus two minutes, inside the profile’s range. Heartbeat and periodic items use fixed limits from their profile; the rows on screen are mostly those. The table always says where each limit came from, so an analyst can trust it or override it on Manage. We have not measured a false-alarm rate at customer scale: the pilot does that, with a goal of 7 days without a false alert (roadmap F4).
