BACKUP · PREREQUISITES

The homework, in eight parts

1 · Splunk Enterprise

The data_health index, retention, KV store, scheduler room for the 1-minute searches.

2 · Roles

dh_analyst and dh_admin, and who may set test blocks.

3 · Splunk agents

_internal forwarded from every agent; a version policy; the inputs per OS.

4 · Cribl Stream

Its own logs in Splunk, its config-change audit, one expected row per worker group.

5 · Hosts

auditd and its rules; Windows Security and System logs; device syslog plus a probe.

6 · Inventory feed

One source of truth for both the inputs and the expected list.

7 · Change process

A marker before each planned change; mutes for maintenance.

8 · Security settings

Keep Splunk’s dashboard risky-command check on.

Checklist with checks and owners: docs/adoption/prerequisites.md
Each item in the full checklist says what it is, why the app needs it, how to check it, and what breaks without it. Two things a customer must change from the lab: the list of watched indexes, and the lab’s host names that ship as macro defaults (the relay name and the probe host).
